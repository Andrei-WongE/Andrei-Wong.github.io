Funded by the Economic and Social Research Council (ESRC), this research interrogates official government metrics and indicator frameworks used to measure wellbeing and multidimensional welfare.

The study explores the trade-offs between subjective wellbeing indicators and objective socioeconomic datasets, reviewing policy applications and monitoring tools across multiple jurisdictions.

### Key Dimensions
- **Indicator Design:** Harmonization of subjective and multidimensional poverty indices.
- **Institutional Architecture:** Structural challenges in official statistical agencies.
- **Sponsorship:** Supported by the Economic and Social Research Council (ESRC) and the Institute of Development Studies (IDS).
