This research provides a systematic empirical review of data availability, survey infrastructures, and statistical governance models used by governments to assess migrant populations. 

The paper examines how administrative records and national survey samples represent mobile populations, highlighting systemic gaps in spatial granularity, longitudinal tracking, and socio-economic integration indicators.

### Key Dimensions
- **Data Governance:** Assessment of institutional registries and census integration.
- **Spatial Resolution:** Analyzing the limits of boundary definitions in capturing migrant residential patterns.
- **Methodology:** Systematic review protocol following PRISMA guidelines across international statistical datasets.
