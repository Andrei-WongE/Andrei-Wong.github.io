Conducted for the World Bank, this study evaluates the macroeconomic and microeconomic transmission channels of COVID-19 containment policies across Peru’s major trade corridors and global value chains.

The analysis integrates firm-level transaction registries with sectoral input-output matrices to estimate vulnerability and recovery pathways.

### Key Dimensions
- **Input-Output Analysis:** Modeling domestic linkages and intermediate input vulnerability.
- **Firm Resilience:** Empirical evaluation of employment preservation and liquidity constraints.
- **Institutional Context:** World Bank policy advisory for post-pandemic industrial resilience.
