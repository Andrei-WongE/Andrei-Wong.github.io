# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Recruiters, hiring committees, funding bodies, research peers, collaborators, and fellow PhD students in urban geography.

## Product Purpose
To showcase PhD research, publications, teaching, talks, and CV, serving as the central hub for academic profile visibility and collaborative outreach.

## Brand Personality
Technical, structured, and data-driven, emphasizing spatial datasets and urban geography.

## Anti-references
Generic AI/SaaS design patterns (e.g., high-contrast dark modes, neon blue/purple gradients, cookie-cutter icon-and-text grids).

## Design Principles
1. **Data-First Structure**: The visual layout should feel clean and structured like a dataset—precise alignments, clean tables, and spatial logic.
2. **Scholarly Rigor**: The style should convey academic authority and depth without reverting to dry, text-heavy browser defaults.
3. **Organic Technicality**: Use the Everforest theme to bridge natural organic tones (greens, warm grays) with precise, grid-based layouts.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Ensure high text-to-background contrast (min 4.5:1), clear keyboard navigation indicators, and strict adherence to prefers-reduced-motion media queries.
